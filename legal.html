Terms, privacy and security

Terms of use

Strumly is a student prototype built for IMY 320. Courses, ratings and instructors are sample content, and lessons do not contain real video. Use the site to explore the design.

Privacy

Strumly has no server. Your email, progress, saved courses and settings are stored only in this browser’s local storage and are never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Security

Because sign-in is simulated, passwords are not checked or stored.
